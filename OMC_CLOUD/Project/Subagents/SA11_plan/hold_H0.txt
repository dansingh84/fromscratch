4. WHICH STAGES MUST CHANGE FOR AN EXACT HOLD (from the measurements above)
 (i)   the residual decision on inter slices: any non-zero index re-codes quantisation noise every frame (the
       57-89 % churn); a zero residual gives an exact hold of prediction, transform, XSL, clip and gamut repair
       (0 changed samples in 3 of 3 cells, repair ON and OFF).
 (ii)  the rolling intra refresh: the intra re-encode differs from the held picture on 61-74 % of the refreshed
       slice (40-90 % of refresh rows per frame) -- the dominant remaining change with zero residuals.
 (iii) the refresh-barrier display blend: row 0 of the slice below and the last row of the slice above a
       refreshed slice change in the DISPLAY only (committed unchanged): 0.9-3.7 % of inter-row samples.
 NOT involved: prediction, pcoef (unquantised), inverse transform, XSL cancellation, clip, gamut repair.
